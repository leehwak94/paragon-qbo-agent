Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Paragon QBO Agent (the “Application”), a private internal accounting automation tool operated for Alarie Solutions Électrique, accesses, uses, stores and protects information.

1. Scope and authorized user

The Application is used only by the authorized QuickBooks Online company administrator and designated internal operator. It is not offered as a public or multi-tenant service.

2. Information accessed

With OAuth authorization, the Application may access QuickBooks Online company information required for supplier-invoice processing, including company information, vendors, chart of accounts, tax codes, existing bills, bill attachments and transaction metadata. It may also process supplier-invoice emails and attachments from the separately authorized company Gmail account.

3. Purposes of use

Information is used only to extract supplier-invoice details, validate vendors and accounting classifications, detect possible duplicates, calculate or validate applicable taxes, prepare approval proposals, create approved supplier bills, attach supporting documents and troubleshoot the internal workflow.

4. Human approval

The Application is designed to require explicit approval from the authorized administrator before creating or modifying accounting entries in QuickBooks Online. It does not initiate payments.

5. Data sharing and artificial intelligence

QuickBooks data is not sold, rented, used for advertising or shared with unaffiliated parties except service providers necessary to operate the authorized integration. QuickBooks data is not used to train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

6. Storage and security

OAuth credentials, access tokens, refresh tokens, extracted invoice data and operational logs are stored on an access-controlled local machine or private controlled environment. Credentials are not embedded in public source code. Reasonable administrative and technical controls are used to restrict access to the authorized operator.

7. Retention

Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for accounting records, validation, audit support, legal obligations and operation of the internal workflow. Temporary files and OAuth authorization codes are deleted or allowed to expire when no longer needed.

8. Revocation and deletion requests

The authorized administrator may revoke QuickBooks access from the Intuit account’s connected-app settings. Requests to disconnect the Application, delete locally stored OAuth tokens, or review retained Application data may be sent to the contact below.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated as the internal workflow or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.

10. Contact
