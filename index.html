Controlled accounting automation.

Paragon is a private internal tool that helps the authorized QuickBooks Online company administrator review supplier invoices, validate accounting information and prepare entries for explicit approval.

Approval first

No accounting entry is posted without explicit approval from the authorized administrator.

Private by design

The application is not offered to the public and is used only for internal company operations.

Data protection

QuickBooks data is not sold, shared for advertising, or used to train artificial intelligence models.
