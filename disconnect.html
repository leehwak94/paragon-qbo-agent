Disconnect & data requests

The authorized company administrator can revoke access at any time.

Disconnect from QuickBooks Online

1
Sign in to the authorized QuickBooks Online administrator account.
2
Open connected applications or app management settings.
3
Locate Paragon QBO Agent and select the option to disconnect or revoke access.

Request local token or data deletion

Email alariebilling@gmail.com with the subject “Paragon data request.” The authorized operator will verify the requester and process the request in accordance with accounting-record and legal-retention obligations.
